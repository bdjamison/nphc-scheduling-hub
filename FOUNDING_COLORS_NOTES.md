# Divine Nine founding-date colors

Founding anniversaries use organization-specific colors on Day, Week and Month calendars. Federal holidays retain their existing blue design. These are informational only and never create bookings or block availability.

Color pairings:
- Alpha Phi Alpha: black and old gold
- Alpha Kappa Alpha: salmon pink and apple green
- Kappa Alpha Psi: crimson and cream
- Omega Psi Phi: purple and gold
- Delta Sigma Theta: crimson and cream
- Phi Beta Sigma: royal blue and white
- Zeta Phi Beta: royal blue and white
- Sigma Gamma Rho: royal blue and gold
- Iota Phi Theta: charcoal brown and gilded gold

Corrected Sigma Gamma Rho founding date to November 12 (1922).

No API, database, or Azure settings changes required.
