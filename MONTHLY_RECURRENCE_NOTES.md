# Monthly recurring reservations

Select Recurring reservation, then Monthly, then 1st/2nd/3rd/4th/Last and a weekday. Choose start/end times and a date range (up to one year). Both availability and submission expand the monthly pattern into dated reservations and check each occurrence for conflicts. The existing email verification, approver workflow, and calendar are unchanged. The public recurring request limit remains 52 occurrences. No fifth-weekday option is provided; Last means the final instance of that weekday each month.

Example: 2nd Wednesday of each month, 6–8 PM. Existing weekly recurring reservations are unchanged.
